Scientific note: This is a simplified illustration of the net bond changes, not a detailed enzyme-catalysed reaction mechanism. The oxygen in the final C1–O–C4 bridge is retained from the second glucose C4 hydroxyl group. The hydroxyl group at C1 of the first glucose and hydrogen from the second hydroxyl are represented as water. The ring diagrams are deliberately simplified rather than complete Haworth projections; each red ring oxygen is at the upper-right vertex, bonded to the adjacent ring carbons rather than placed inside the ring.
