How water can be lost from a planet

Ultraviolet radiation can break water molecules into hydrogen-containing fragments. Lightweight hydrogen can then escape from the upper atmosphere. Adjust the illustrative upper-atmosphere heating / escape activity slider to explore the idea.

Water loss on Mars also involves UV chemistry, solar-wind-driven escape and loss of oxygen; escape velocity alone does not explain the difference. Earth also loses some hydrogen.
