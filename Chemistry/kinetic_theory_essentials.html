Kinetic theory of matter

Explore the motion and arrangement of particles in solids, liquids and gases. Move the energy slider to see how the particles respond.

4.0

This slider changes motion within each state; it does not automatically change a substance from solid to liquid to gas. The three panels are simplified particle models, not a molecular-scale measurement.

Solid

Fixed positions; particles vibrate

Particles remain near fixed positions and vibrate. Strong interactions maintain a definite shape and volume.

Liquid

Close together; particles rearrange

Particles stay close but continually change neighbours. A liquid has a fixed volume but takes the shape of its container.

Gas

Far apart; rapid random motion

Particles move throughout the available space, with collisions and large separations. A gas fills its container and is readily compressed.
