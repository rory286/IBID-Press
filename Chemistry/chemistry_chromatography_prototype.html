The three Rf values are illustrative, not measurements from a particular experiment. The solvent front is assumed to move steadily, and each dye's distance is modelled as a fixed fraction of the solvent distance.

Teaching point: different affinities for the stationary phase (paper and its associated water) and mobile phase (solvent) cause the components to travel different distances. The starting line must remain above the solvent level in the reservoir.
